What is Jiu-Jitsu?

Leverage over strength

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a grappling martial art that teaches how to control distance, use leverage, and work toward safer positions, including when facing a larger or stronger opponent.

Training includes movement, escapes, positional control, takedowns, and submissions. Students practice techniques with a partner in a structured, supervised setting.

Why train?

What you get on the mat

  • Improve strength, coordination, and overall fitness.
  • Practice focus, patience, and problem-solving.
  • Build confidence through progress you can feel.
  • Train with respect, teamwork, and encouragement.
